Our Mission

Bringing AI to Burundian Farmers

Smart Agriculture AI is a national agricultural decision support platform built to help farmers across Burundi detect crop diseases early, access planting calendars, and receive weather-aware farming advice — all in one place.

The Problem

Crop disease costs Burundian farmers up to 40% of their harvest

In Burundi, over 80% of the population depends on agriculture for food and income. Yet diseases like Bean Rust, Cassava Mosaic, and Coffee Leaf Rust spread undetected because farmers lack access to agronomists and diagnostic tools. By the time a disease is identified, it has often destroyed the entire crop.

The Solution

AI-powered diagnosis in seconds

A farmer photographs their crop with any smartphone. Our AI vision model — Google Gemini 2.5 Flash — analyses the image and returns a full diagnosis: disease name, confidence score, severity level, and concrete treatment steps. No agronomist required. No internet subscription needed beyond a basic data connection.

Crops Supported

8 key crops. All major Burundian staples.

Common Bean (Ibishyimbo) Maize (Ibigori) Cassava (Imanioka) Arabica Coffee (Ikawa) Banana (Igitoke) Sweet Potato (Ibijumba) Rice (Umuceri) Sorghum (Amasaka)

Platform Features

More than just disease detection

  • AI crop disease diagnosis from photos
  • Real-time weather with farming risk assessment
  • Planting and harvest calendar for all seasons
  • Disease reference library with treatment guides
  • Agronomic recommendations per diagnosis
  • GPS-based local weather detection

Data Sources

Built on trusted agricultural science

Crop calendar and disease data is sourced from ISABU (Institut des Sciences Agronomiques du Burundi), FAO Burundi country reports, and regional research from CIALCA (Consortium for Improving Agriculture-based Livelihoods in Central Africa). Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo, an open-source weather API.

Technology

Modern, open, and free to access

Built with Python and Flask. AI analysis powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash. Weather data from Open-Meteo (no subscription required). Geocoding from OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Designed mobile-first for smartphone use in the field.
